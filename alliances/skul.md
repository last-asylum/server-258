# [Skul] Skull
Last Asylum: Plague - Server #258 - Skul

Welcome to the Skull Guild! Please note the following:
1. Follow [Server #258 Rules](../README.md#rules)
2. Donate to Alliance Research
    - tap Donate (Herbs, right) until no more attempts remain
    - tap Donate (diamonds, left) if you feel generous
3. Assist other members to reduce their construction, research, training and healing times
4. Rally to defeat Elite Blight for Alliance Gifts
5. Exchange items at the Alliance Shop weekly
6. Please rename yourself from "Doctor..." to something meaningful and relatable for human "consumption"! 😎

We recommend putting shields up every week from Saturday evening to Sunday morning - so there is protection against potential attacks from other servers' players.

## Important:

Please follow the grid layout we've set up at the Alliance Assembly Point!

## Ranks:

Open for suggestions and discussion but the current guideline is the following:

- **`R5`** - Alliance Leader
- **`R4`** - Alliance Management: at least Lv.20 and 15 million might
- **`R3`** - Active players XL: at least Lv.15 and 10 million might
- **`R2`** - Active, new & small players
- **`R1`** - Inactive players - everyone who is away for 7 days or more

## New joiners:

- move close to the Alliance Assembly Point,
- promote to R2.

## Inactivity:

Being offline for 7 days or more will result in losing rank: member will be demoted to **`R1`**.

If you will be away for longer, please let someone from **`R4`** know - alternatively **`Diplomat`**, **`Goddess`**, **`Recruiter`**, *`God of War`** or **`Leader`**
